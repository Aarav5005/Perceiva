## The narrative shift
OLD site leads with classroom intelligence ("reads attention, detects struggle in
real time", "Perceiva sees what teachers can't", IT SEES / IT UNDERSTANDS /
IT REMEMBERS, "currently building with founding schools", "this is real, running now").
NEW site leads with interactive teaching (Pillar A): ask a question → get a living,
scientifically grounded visual explanation. Classroom intelligence becomes a short,
clearly-labeled secondary research section. Every unconfirmed claim removed.
